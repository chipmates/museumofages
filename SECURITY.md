# Security

If you find a security problem in Museum of Ages, tell us quietly first.

## How to report

Reports are read by ChipMates gemeinnützige GmbH, which runs the museum. Write to [contact@museumofages.org](mailto:contact@museumofages.org) with "Security" in the subject. Please do not open a public issue for it. Give us time to fix it, and we agree a date with you before anyone else is told.

Say what you found, where it is, and how we can see it for ourselves. A screenshot or a short proof helps. Neither is required.

## What happens next

We confirm that your report arrived within three working days, and tell you within two weeks what we make of it. When it is fixed, we tell you, and we say what was fixed in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md). If you want, we name you there. If you would rather stay unnamed, we keep it that way.

## What is in scope

Only the live site and the main branch of this repository get fixes.


- museumofages.org and its pages
- the museum itself: the lobby, the wing and the film player
- the code in this repository

## What is out of scope

- The sites of the museums, libraries and collections a label links to. Please report to them.
- Our host's own systems. The site runs on Cloudflare. Please report to Cloudflare.
- Agora Cosmica, our other project. It has [its own security policy](https://github.com/chipmates/agoracosmica/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## What there is to protect

The museum has no accounts and no logins. It sets no tracking cookies and does no profiling. The wing keeps a few notes in your browser's storage, such as your sound setting and the stops you stood at, and they stay on your device. The [privacy page](https://museumofages.org/privacy/) lists them all.
